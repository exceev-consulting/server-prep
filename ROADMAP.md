# Roadmap

The goal: someone with no DevOps background ends with a hardened server, connected to their panel,
and an audit with 0 FAIL.

The work is split into phases. Each phase is a milestone:

- **Phase 0: Foundation**: [milestone](https://github.com/exceev-consulting/server-prep/milestone/1)
- **Phase 1: Community & CI**: [milestone](https://github.com/exceev-consulting/server-prep/milestone/2)
- **Phase 2: Panels** (Openship to the level of Coolify): [milestone](https://github.com/exceev-consulting/server-prep/milestone/3)
- **Phase 3: Beginner agent install**: [milestone](https://github.com/exceev-consulting/server-prep/milestone/4)
- **Phase 4: Capability page** (the setup console): [milestone](https://github.com/exceev-consulting/server-prep/milestone/5)

Bug fixes go in the [Next patch release](https://github.com/exceev-consulting/server-prep/milestone/6) milestone.
